Designing a Central Place for Buyers and Sellers to Track Their Transactions

Overview

A transaction overview page for Kleinanzeigen's C2C payment experience — one clear interface showing ongoing and past transactions in one place. Both buyers and sellers needed a way to track what was happening with their marketplace transactions without having to piece it together from individual conversations.

My role

Senior Product Designer. I designed the transaction overview experience across Android, iOS, and web.

The challenge

Before this, there was no dedicated place for users to see the status of their transactions. Buyers and sellers had to navigate to individual chats — which worked for a single transaction but broke down fast for anyone managing more than one at a time.

On top of the UX gap, DAC7 — the EU regulation requiring platforms to report seller income — introduced a new compliance requirement: sellers needed a structured, accessible record of their transaction activity.

Goals

  • A dedicated overview page as a central hub for all transaction activity
  • Clear separation between ongoing and past transactions
  • DAC7 tracking support for sellers
  • A structure that serves both the buyer and seller journey
  • A scalable foundation for future wallet features

Research findings

The overview was new to most users but received strongly positive — particularly by high-volume and PRO sellers who had previously managed everything through a cluttered message inbox.

DAC7 compliance was a genuine user need, not just a regulatory checkbox. Sellers actively used the overview to monitor how close they were to the reporting threshold.

The clearest point of confusion: users who hadn't used Kleinanzeigen's integrated payment feature expected the overview to show their entire sales history on the platform, not just in-app transactions.

What this shaped

The research findings directly influenced the design priorities: surfacing pending amounts alongside completed totals, adding a sales counter tied to the DAC7 30-item threshold, and structuring the page to make the scope of what's included immediately clear.
