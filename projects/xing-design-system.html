Design System · XING · 2019

Consolidating Three Design Systems Into One Cross-Platform Foundation

"A complex system that works is invariably found to have evolved from a simple system that works." — John Gall

Context

When XING expanded from a single Hamburg office to a multi-office European organization, its design practice scaled without a shared foundation. Three separate design systems had emerged organically across web, iOS, and Android teams — each internally consistent, none of them talking to the others.

My role

Senior UX Designer, core contributor to the design system workstream. I was involved from the audit phase through to the foundations definition and initial component library build. My focus was the interface inventory, the semantic color and typography systems, and the motion foundation.

Process

We started with a full interface inventory — a systematic audit of every component, pattern, and visual decision across all three systems. The inventory surfaced the redundancy and gave us a shared vocabulary for the consolidation conversations.

From there, we worked foundation-up: define the tokens before designing the components, define the components before assembling the patterns.

Foundations defined

  • Typography — unified type scale with semantic role assignments that mapped to platform-native equivalents
  • Iconography — consolidated icon library with consistent sizing, stroke weight, and optical alignment rules
  • Semantic color palette — role-based color tokens enabling theming and future brand updates
  • Spacing scale — 4px base grid with named steps, shared across all three platforms
  • Motion — easing curves, duration ranges, and usage principles for transitions and micro-interactions

What I learned

A design system is primarily a negotiation product. The technical decisions are rarely the hard part — getting three platform teams, each with legitimate reasons for their existing patterns, to agree on a shared direction without feeling overridden requires transparency about trade-offs, not just better components.
