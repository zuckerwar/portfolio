Scope & scale

  • Platforms: Android, iOS, web — designed for all three in parallel, not adapted from one
  • Scale: products used by millions of users across Germany's largest C2C marketplace
  • Cross-functional reach: product, engineering, legal, trust & safety, brand, and design leadership
  • Org influence: token architecture, component contribution models, governance processes, and documentation standards adopted across multiple product teams
  • Problem types: ambiguous, cross-team, spanning both system infrastructure and consumer product

What I focus on

  • Scaling design systems with token-based architecture across Android, iOS, and web
  • Designing transaction flows that build trust and improve conversion in large-scale marketplaces
  • Reducing the distance between design decisions and engineering implementation
  • Creating documentation and contribution models that keep systems alive after handoff
  • Running cross-functional workshops that align product, engineering, and design on shared direction

Working with AI

AI is a constant part of my day-to-day. I use it throughout the design process — ideation and concept exploration, writing and iterating on UX copy, building prototypes to test ideas quickly, and making code changes directly when that's the faster path. I use Claude as my primary tool, alongside others depending on what the task needs.

The point isn't the tools — it's the shift in where I spend my time. AI handles the things that can be accelerated. That frees me up for the judgment calls, the hard tradeoffs, and the cross-functional work that actually requires a designer in the room.

How I work

I tend to work closest to the seams — the places where design meets development, where systems meet product, where a clean component library meets a genuinely messy real-world problem. I'm comfortable holding ambiguity, but I push for clarity in outcomes. I care about impact numbers not because they're required for case studies, but because they tell you whether the work actually worked.

I also care about craft at the detail level: the right motion curve, the token name that's obvious six months later, the edge case that nobody else put on the spec.

Currently

Based in Hamburg. Open to staff-level roles in design systems, product design, or design operations — environments where the work involves shaping how teams make decisions, not just delivering outputs. Ideally complex, multi-platform, and at a scale where system quality has a measurable product impact.
