About

Yaara Zuckerwar

Senior Product Designer — Design Systems & Product

I'm a product designer with a focus on design systems, complex product flows, and the collaboration between design and engineering. I've spent the last several years building and scaling systems for large platforms — working across mobile, web, and the spaces in between.

Before that, I was an animator. I spent several years in broadcast — character animation, title sequences, motion graphics. That background shapes how I think about interaction: motion as communication, not decoration.

I care about the infrastructure of design as much as the surface. Naming conventions, contribution models, documentation culture — these aren't soft concerns. They're what determines whether a design system actually gets used.

Focus areas

Systems

Building the token architecture, contribution models, and governance processes that let multiple teams work from one source of truth.

Product

Designing complex product flows across platforms — from the user problem through to the decision tradeoffs and the shipped experience.

Alignment

Closing the gap between design and engineering — through specs, shared decision frameworks, and the kind of cross-functional work that makes quality durable.
